Connect your WordPress site with Quasar AI SEO to publish AI-generated content directly from your Quasar dashboard.

== Description ==

Quasar AI SEO Assistant connects your WordPress site with the Quasar AI SEO platform, allowing you to:

* Publish AI-generated blog posts directly to WordPress from your Quasar dashboard
* View all posts created via Quasar AI SEO in a beautiful dashboard
* Track published, draft, and scheduled posts
* One-click connection setup
* Automatic featured image support
* Category and tag management

== Installation ==

1. Upload the `quasar-ai-seo-assistant` folder to `/wp-content/plugins/`
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu in WordPress
3. Go to the "Quasar AI SEO" menu in your WordPress admin
4. Click "Connect to Quasar AI SEO" to establish the connection
5. Start creating posts from https://seo.quasarasoft.com/post-create

== Requirements ==

* WordPress 5.6 or higher (for Application Passwords support)
* PHP 7.4 or higher
* HTTPS enabled on your site
* A Quasar AI SEO account (sign up at https://seo.quasarasoft.com)
